How college actually works?

No fluff. Here's the real deal about classes, exams, attendance, and keeping your CGPA alive.

Branches offered at Amrita Amaravati
Pick your lane and own it. Here's what's available:

📝
Exam pattern — CIA + ESE
  • CIA = 70 marks (2 unit tests + assignments + quizzes + mid(20 marks)) + LABS
  • ESE = 30 marks (2-hour end semester exam)


  • Marks distribution might be varied according to the course
  • below 75% attendance = you're barred from ESE. no exceptions.
📈
How to not ruin your CGPA
  • don't underestimate so-called "easy" subjects
  • form a study group in the very first week
  • revise a little every day — not just before CIA
  • visit professors during office hours when stuck
  • a 9+ CGPA opens research, exchange, and placement doors

05 — survival kit

stuff seniors wish
they'd known. 🛟

the unwritten rules. the mistakes that cost people their CGPA. the things that take most freshers a full semester to figure out.

✓ do these things
  • always carry your ID card. everywhere. always.
  • introduce yourself to professors in week 1
  • join at least one tech + one cultural club
  • keep a planner for assignment deadlines
  • build a daily 1-hour revision habit from day 1
  • attend orientation events — that's where you meet your people
  • talk to 2nd & 3rd year seniors. they know everything.
  • save lab records before the deadline, not after
✗ avoid these mistakes
  • don't skip classes in your first month — habits set fast
  • don't ignore CIA dates — missing one tanks your grade
  • don't track attendance only at week 8. too late.
  • don't take on too many commitments in sem 1
  • don't sacrifice sleep. 6 hours minimum. always.
  • don't leave lab records to the night before
  • don't compare yourself to toppers — just beat yesterday you
  • don't be afraid to ask "dumb" questions in class. everyone's lost.
⏱️
time management
try the 50/10 rule — 50 mins focus, 10 min break. batch similar tasks on the same day. protect sunday mornings for a weekly review. 2 hours focused > 6 hours distracted.
🧠
mental health
homesickness is normal and temporary. everyone's feeling it. call home as much as you need to. your mind is your most important resource.
🤝
build your people
college friends become professional references. help others in labs & assignments. your batch is your support system for the next 4 years. invest in those relationships from day 1.

❓ FAQ — things everyone asks
How many times can I go out? +
You have monthly
2 Outpasses, 2 Homepasses
& Emergency pass - Emergency purposes only
When will I get ID card & uniform +
It usually takes time, but you will get within your first semester exam.
When should I start prepping for placements? +
start building DSA on leetcode from sem 3, and your github portfolio from sem 2. placements happen in year 4 but the prep takes 2 years. don't panic, but don't wait either.
what laptop should I bring? +
minimum 8GB RAM (16 is better), i5/ryzen 5 or above, 256GB+ SSD. battery life and portability matter more than GPU for most CSE coursework. don't buy a gaming laptop just to look cool.
